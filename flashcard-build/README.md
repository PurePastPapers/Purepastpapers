# Flashcard rebuild

All five requested subjects are published and verified: AQA A-level Psychology (806 cards), AQA A-level Sociology (567), Edexcel A-level English Literature (435), OCR GCSE History B (482) and Eduqas GCSE English Literature (349). Together they contain 2,639 cards in 17 catalogues. See `publication-results.json` and `live-verification.json` for the recorded release and verification details.

GCSE History excludes History Around Us and Verulamium. A-level English Literature follows the confirmed text route and excludes NEA. GCSE English Literature uses the 15-poem anthology first assessed in 2027. Both English sets include quotation recall as well as context and technique cards.

`give this to will.md` is the current final handover, with a fresh seeded sample of 222 cards. Earlier checkpoint samples, recovery reports and draft notes are historical. Subject folders contain the sources, reviews, builds and audit records for each release; the original Psychology and Sociology MARK identities and fronts were preserved.
