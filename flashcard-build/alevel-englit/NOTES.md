# A-level English Literature draft

Status: DRAFT. Not ready for approval or publication. Cards and sources need independent full verification and a random 10% audit; this has not been done. The source ledger marks draft records as unverified. The short independent review of core and technique found several ambiguous cards; five were removed, two further cards removed, and other points require review. No error rate is claimed.

The requested route is Othello, A Streetcar Named Desire, Frankenstein, The Handmaid's Tale, the 20 listed Poems of the Decade and the 19 listed Romantic poems. The modern and Romantic cards focus on textual moments and methods. This extends the proposed Key quotations deck: it does not yet provide the quotation recall coverage its title promises. A small set of Othello quotes uses Folger's text, not necessarily the student's edition. No in-copyright passages are quoted in the cards. The modern poem cards draw on Pearson international assessment materials covering those same poems. They require full independent review against the actual works. Romantic cards were checked by their author against online texts, not against the prescribed Wright anthology, and require independent checking and edition comparison. These sets are not a substitute for the prescribed books.

The critical-reading deck is preliminary. All four works need wider thematic and contextual coverage; modern and Romantic poem leaves need more depth, and unseen skills are not yet represented as atomic cards. Prose coverage is also thin. Context and criticism are not comprehensive. Model answers are illustrative short paragraphs, not official model answers. Paper structure is sourced from Pearson's specification and technique from the public sample assessment materials. Some historical claims and critic paraphrases still require confirmation.

Sources are in draft-sources.tsv keyed by front; draft means not independently verified. The exact stable key will be produced by the shared compiler after source checking. Do not treat the existence of a URL as proof that a card was verified. No in-copyright quotation is listed for Will because none is intentionally included; verify this during review. No final 10% audit or publishable sources.tsv exists yet.
