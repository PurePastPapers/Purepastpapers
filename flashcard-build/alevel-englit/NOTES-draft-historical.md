# A-level English Literature draft

Status: draft, not independently reviewed or ready for publication. The route includes all 45 specified leaves. A leaf with a card is not proof of comprehensive preparation. The shared draft compiler and site tag mapper pass. The source ledger records 128 previously checked and 320 awaiting independent review; every new card is marked no. Do not use a successful draft build as a claim of accuracy or readiness.

The quotation deck retains textual moments and methods, in addition to quotation-recall cards. Each of the 19 Romantic poems and each of the 20 contemporary poems has at least one short quotation anchor. This is not comprehensive quotation coverage: many texts have only one anchor, and the novel and play anchors are especially sparse. The copyrighted anchors appear in COPYRIGHT-QUOTES-TO-CHECK.md. Per work, the wording reproduced is kept within the allowed short-quotation budget; additional breadth requires user-supplied text or appropriate permission. Do not add unverified quotations to fill gaps.

Each public-domain anchor cites the opened edition. Folger and the online editions are not necessarily identical to the student’s prescribed editions; Wright’s English Romantic Verse edition needs comparison. Modern anchors include Pearson source-booklet texts, published quotations and other online texts, so the specified anthology must be checked before approval. The Streetcar and Handmaid anchors were checked in secondary excerpts, not the full prescribed works. New recall, referent and interpretation cards require an independent checker, including uniqueness and whether the text supports each interpretation.

Context and critics have been expanded, including Mellor, Howells, Weiss, Snyder and Spivack, but the four longer works, Romantic contexts and 20 contemporary poems still require fuller source-backed coverage. There are seven unseen terminology cards; they are not a complete technique syllabus. Technique answers are brief revision models, not official model answers. A second checker should verify each new card and exact quotation; no new 10% audit or complete-coverage review has been performed. No sample drawn from this revision should be represented as a completed subject until that work is done.
