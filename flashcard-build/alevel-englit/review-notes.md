# Independent English Literature card review

## Scope and result

Reviewed the compiled draft against `EDX_ALEVEL_ENGLIT_9ET0` and the supplied per-card source ledger. The build contains 202 cards, across 44 of 45 spec leaves; `EDX_ENGLIT_MODERN_UNSEEN` remains uncovered because the anthology contains no unseen-poem text to verify. The build compiles without errors. The card-level audit is in `sources.tsv`: `yes` means the factual claim was checked against the cited primary text or the relevant official Pearson source; `no` means the source available did not support a sufficiently precise check. A title match or a mark scheme mentioning a poem was not counted as verification.

## Edits and removals

- Removed the unsupported *Eat Me* title-allusion question and the prior interpretation of regular stanza form as inevitability. Added the text-checkable tercet-form question instead.
- Removed two uncertain *Out of the Bag* claims (a profession associated with Epidaurus and a magician comparison) rather than teach an unchecked reading.
- Removed the *On Her Blindness* colour and couplet-pattern claims because the available source did not establish those textual details.
- Reworded the Byron skull-cup prompt to ask what the speaker invites the reader to do; it now tests the poem’s direct invitation to drink rather than an inferred fear of death.
- Reworded the Bradley card to reflect his account of Iago's attraction to the destructive project and his rationalising motives, avoiding the misleading claim that Bradley simply rejects revenge as a motive.
- Clarified the *To My Nine-Year-Old Self* tree prompt and replaced a broad tone inference in *The Furthest Distances I’ve Travelled* with a direct claim about distance between people.

## Verification limits and follow-up

- Most Romantic cards were checked against the linked full poem text; sources.tsv records this at poem level. The sources are public editions and may differ in punctuation or lineation from Pearson's specified anthology edition.
- Othello plot and language cards were checked against the Folger text and synopsis. Frankenstein cards were checked against the Project Gutenberg full text. The Streetcar and Handmaid's Tale cards have precise text questions but their available citations are specification/exam commentary rather than the plays/novel themselves, so they remain `no` pending direct primary-text checks.
- The five modern-poetry leaves marked verified were checked against the relevant Pearson official exam or mark-scheme material. Other modern-poetry cards remain `no`: I did not treat International A-level 8WET01 commentary as a substitute for checking each claim against the set poem text. They need a rights-cleared or licensed anthology edition check before being marked verified.
- The 14 exam-technique cards were checked against Pearson's 9ET0 specification/assessment information. The four model prompts are revision guidance, not reproduced official mark-scheme answers; their marking points are deliberately generic.
