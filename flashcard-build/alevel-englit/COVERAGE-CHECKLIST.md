# Coverage checklist for independent review

This is a map of the draft, not a claim that the whole subject has passed review. Each named area has at least one card; the checker must decide whether each card is accurate and sufficient.

- Othello: promotion and deception, reputation, racial and gender tensions, jealousy, handkerchief, soliloquy and dramatic irony, tragedy, Cyprus and staging history. Folger provides text and scene summaries.
- A Streetcar Named Desire: Blanche, Stanley, Stella and Mitch; desire, the Old South, class and gender, violence, illusion, loss and mental vulnerability; light, polka, gunshot, song, confined setting and plastic theatre. Pearson mark schemes are sources for the staging claims; the play itself still needs edition checking.
- The Handmaid’s Tale: Offred, Commander, Serena Joy, Nick, Moira, Aunt Lydia and Ofglen; theocratic control, naming, fertility, language, resistance, biblical parallels, recordings and Historical Notes, and uncertainty at the ending. Sources include Penguin, Pearson, COVE and an A-level guide; the prescribed novel is still needed for direct checks.
- Frankenstein: Victor, creature, Walton, Justine, Elizabeth, Clerval, De Laceys and Safie; embedded frames, education, isolation, rejection, responsibility, reproduction, revenge, Alpine sublime and scientific context. Romantic Circles gives an 1818 chapter-indexed summary; quotations cite opened 1818 passages.
- Poems of the Decade: all 20 prescribed leaves retain events/methods and have a brief quotation anchor; each has at least one form, language or development card. Some rely on Pearson mark schemes or secondary transcriptions rather than the prescribed full text.
- The Romantics: all 19 leaves retain textual moments and have a short quotation anchor; form, language or development is represented for each. Public texts and edition locations are recorded, but the Wright anthology has not been compared.
- Unseen poetry: literary terminology has cards; comparison and question-specific skills are also addressed in the separate technique material.

All new cards are marked no in sources.tsv until someone other than the author independently checks the relevant work. All 22 in-copyright quotation anchors are individually listed for Will. There is one anchor per copyrighted work here: the copyright budget prevents pretending it is comprehensive textual evidence. User-supplied material or permission is needed for a broader quotation bank. The full set still needs an independent audit, a quality recheck, a fixed-seed sample and Will’s approval.
