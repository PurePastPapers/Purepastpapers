# Independent review of GCSE English Literature

The 329-card draft has 99 quotation cards, 206 context/method cards and 24 technique cards. The compiler reports no errors and 20 of 20 route leaves represented. The site tag mapper reports 305 correctly mapped atomic cards, 24 technique cards, and zero bad cards. `sources.tsv` and `all-front-sources.tsv` contain a source and a yes verdict for each current stable key. Interpretive answers are defensible readings, not the only valid readings.

The official 2027 Eduqas anthology was opened and cards checked against all 15 poems; the old anthology is not used. Macbeth material was checked against the Folger text and scene links, with Folger's historical records for James I. A Christmas Carol material was checked against the ibiblio text, and Parliament and the British Library for the Poor Law and publication date. Inspector material was checked against official Eduqas extracts, Pearson and AQA published mark schemes or resources, and the AQA-approved Cambridge sample. The two previously uncertain Sheila fragments were confirmed in Pearson documents. The quotations remain on the copyright check list for Will. Exam structure and the method cards were compared with the current Eduqas specification, assessment grids, unseen-poetry guide and Academy of American Poets glossary. The paper-specific comparison of the first anthology question's 15 marks uses Eduqas's published assessment comparison, not an assumed allocation.

Required areas checked for presence: Macbeth kingship, witches, gender, tragedy, marriage and Jacobean context; Inspector Gerald, Eric, generational conflict, dramatic irony, structure and 1912/1945 setting; Carol redemption, poverty, supernatural, Christian charity and Poor Law; each anthology poem has its own text-linked anchor and interpretation or structure; unseen cards cover speaker, imagery, figurative language, pauses, line breaks, stanza and comparison. The cards are a revision aid and do not exhaust every valid interpretation or every fact about the texts. Named topics are covered, but leaf presence is not by itself proof a student can answer every possible question.

Defects repaired in this pass: replaced the unsupported question claiming a character discovered the bodies of Macduff's family with the sourced report to Macduff; removed three tautologies or repeated facts and another three duplicates; clarified the Sheila labour prompt; rewrote recurring abstract poetry prompts and vague recall prompts with concrete textual cues; corrected a daffodil personification prompt and two hedged method answers. Original source ledgers remain historical; current sources are in sources.tsv. The compiler's current source digest is recorded in build-report.json. The owner's separate random audit and fresh stratified sample are still required before any release.

## Parent audit and whole-area correction

The parent reviewer’s initial fixed-seed audit identified 3 defects in 33 cards (9.09% combined quality/source interpretation): two unspecific unseen-poetry prompts and a photograph answer that referred to comparing responses. The entire unseen-method area was reviewed; generic or circular prompts were removed or replaced by specific, glossary-sourced terms while retaining explicit analysis guidance. The Inspector photograph question now asks what uncertainty is created by showing it separately; Pearson’s public 2018 mark scheme (PDF p. 8) supports the possibility that it was not the same image. No other Inspector photograph cards were found. A Remains answer now says the speaker passes the blood-shadow during subsequent patrols. Folger Act 5 Scene 1 confirms the punctuation of the retained Lady Macbeth excerpt; it was not a defect. A new parent sample with a different seed remains required.
