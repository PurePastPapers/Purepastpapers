Q: What is a simile?
A: A comparison using “like” or “as”
Q: What is a metaphor?
A: A description that treats one thing as another
Q: What is personification?
A: Giving human qualities to a non-human thing
Q: What is enjambment?
A: A sentence continuing across a line break
Q: What is a caesura?
A: A pause within a line
Q: What is a stanza?
A: A grouped section of lines in a poem
Q: What is the speaker of a poem?
A: The voice presented in the poem
Q: What is a semantic field?
A: A group of words linked by meaning

## TECHNIQUE | mark
Q: How does Shakespeare present Macbeth’s ambition in the play as a whole?
A: Model: Macbeth’s desire for power leads him to murder Duncan. Marking points: support a developed interpretation and analyse Shakespeare’s methods. Trap: retelling the plot.
Q: How does the writer present responsibility in An Inspector Calls?
A: Model: Sheila accepts responsibility for her treatment of Eva. Marking points: use the extract and whole play; analyse dramatic methods. Trap: ignoring the extract.
Q: How does Dickens present Scrooge’s transformation in A Christmas Carol?
A: Model: Scrooge’s generosity at the end reverses his earlier selfishness. Marking points: support a reading across the novel and analyse methods in context. Trap: recounting events alone.
