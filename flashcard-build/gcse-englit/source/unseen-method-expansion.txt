## EDUQAS_GCSE_ENGLIT_UNSEEN_C.1 | context
Q: What is alliteration?
A: Repetition of consonant sounds, especially at the beginnings of words
Q: What is assonance?
A: Repetition of similar vowel sounds
Q: What is an end-stopped line?
A: A line ending at a pause or complete unit of sense
Q: What is a refrain in a poem?
A: A phrase or line repeated at intervals
Q: What is anaphora?
A: Repetition at the start of successive phrases or lines
Q: What is tone in a poem?
A: The attitude conveyed towards the subject or audience
Q: When analysing a poem’s imagery, what should a response explain?
A: The effect of the selected words
Q: What is the weakness of naming a technique without explaining it?
A: It does not explain how meaning is created
Q: When a poem addresses someone, what is useful to consider?
A: The effect of the chosen addressee
