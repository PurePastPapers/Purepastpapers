# Independent review, 25 November 2026

Reviewed the draft written by another agent. The 2027 Eduqas anthology was opened and each retained anthology card checked against its poem. Interpretations are supported readings, not exclusive answers. I cut 15 unspecific prompts asking for a detail that supports a poem's concerns: each admitted many unrelated answers. I also cut the Schoolboy and Drummer Hodge theme cards, whose answers described an image or event rather than a theme, and a vague tone-effect card. See audit-cuts.txt for all 18 fronts. I corrected the punctuation of the Lady Macbeth quotation to match the Folger edition; this changes its draft stable key. Macbeth cards were compared with Folger's play and scene summaries.

The resulting draft has 122 cards and covers 20 of 20 leaves, but coverage is thin and is not proof of completeness. The common compiler reports no structural errors, and the site's tag mapper returns zero bad cards (107 atomic, 15 technique). Sources.tsv records 85 verified cards and 37 unverified cards with stable keys. No claim of a 10% audit error rate is made. Unverified cards include A Christmas Carol, most An Inspector Calls, poetry terminology and model answers: their leads did not all substantiate the exact facts when opened. Continue source checking or cut them before approval. Text-specific additions need review by someone other than their author. No in-copyright quotations were added.

Subsequent whole-file review: the parent's sample identified systematic overly broad prompts, so I removed 59 further voice, situation, theme and author-name templates. I also cut the combined Scrooge quotation because the source does not present it as a contiguous utterance, and corrected Tiny Tim's quotation to match the spoken wording. This changes its draft stable key. A primary text hosted at ibiblio was opened after Gutenberg returned 503, allowing the remaining Dickens cards to be checked; the Eduqas materials and the Academy of American Poets glossary confirmed further cards. I then authored 14 targeted replacement questions for the eight contemporary poems; they remain unverified by an independent reviewer and are marked no in the ledger. They do not replace a thorough poem build.

Current draft: 76 cards, 20/20 leaves with at least one card, 41 independently checked and 35 pending, including every replacement. Compiler and tag mapper pass. The earlier counts above record the intermediate stage only. The high defect rate in the small sample means the full subject still requires a complete quality recheck, source verification and new sample before approval. All 20 leaves having a card is not evidence of sufficient coverage.
