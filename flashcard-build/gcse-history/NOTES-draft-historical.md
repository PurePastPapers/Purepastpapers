# OCR GCSE History B J411 build notes

## Scope

This draft covers the 21 non-History-Around-Us leaves in the OCR J411/14 and J411/33 routes: Crime and Punishment, the Norman Conquest, Viking Expansion and Living under Nazi Rule. Verulamium / History Around Us is explicitly excluded from the current compiler source; its prior draft files remain under `archive-verulamium/`.

## Current draft status

The compiler reports 477 cards across 21/21 required leaves (388 exam knowledge, 66 background, 23 mark-scheme technique) with no structural errors. The History Around Us leaves are excluded. The 63 additions in `source/completeness.txt` target the topic gaps listed in `completeness-independent.md`; they are indexed in `coverage-checklist.md` and each has a source, page/line note and author verification in `new-cards-for-review.tsv` and `sources.tsv`. They still require independent card-level review before approval.

An additional Nazi completeness file is authored and reviewed separately; its source records are handled in the matching review files. Do not treat an author “yes” as an independent verdict.
