**50. Exam technique** (`anki-card:1505511429137`)

- Front: Assess the claim that globalisation shows religion is becoming more significant. State one supporting argument, one qualification and a judgement.
- Back: Model answer Global communication and migration can spread religions and enable transnational communities, supporting the claim that religion remains influential. Yet the visibility or spread of religion does not by itself show that overall participation or institutional authority is increasing; secularisation may occur unevenly across societies and dimensions.
