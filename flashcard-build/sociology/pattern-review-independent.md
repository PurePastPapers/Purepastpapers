# Independent atomic wording pattern scan

26 November 2026. Inspected all 540 atomic front/back pairs in the current draft, including a search for categorical language (all, always, never, only, every, solely, must, cause, prove, cannot). This is a targeted pattern scan, not a second source audit of every card. Specific matters referred to the author for correction and re-verification:

1. Beanpole family definition omitted the small number of people in each generation. OCR's A Level Sociology families delivery guide defines multiple generations with few children in each: https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/182947-families-and-relationships-delivery-guide.pdf.
2. General experiment strength should identify laboratory experiments when using controlled variables as the characteristic strength, because designs differ in their control of variables.
3. Interpretivism answer stated categorically that meanings cannot be studied like natural objects; replacing with attributed actor-meaning emphasis avoids overstating a heterogeneous perspective. See https://opentextbc.ca/introductiontosociology3rdedition/chapter/1-3-theoretical-perspectives/.
4. Becker question asking whose perspective sociologists 'usually' take overgeneralises the essay and can conflate his hierarchy of credibility with sociologists' choices. Primary: https://www.sfu.ca/~palys/Becker1967-WhoseSideAreWeOn.pdf. Becker discusses sociologists siding with subordinates, while hierarchies confer credibility on superordinates; it is not a simple factual reversal.
5. Family scope: specific childbearing causes already appear in current cards (contraception, higher education, and falling child mortality), while explicit divorce/separation explanation was not found across the atomic fronts.
