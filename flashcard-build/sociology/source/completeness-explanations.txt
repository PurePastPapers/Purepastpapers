A: More widespread use of contraception

A: Greater participation in higher education

A: Mass vaccination programmes

A: Better sanitation
Q: Which political change coincided with increased EU net migration after 2004 according to ONS?
A: Expansion of the European Union

A: Women may have fewer babies

## AQA_SOC_4.3.1_b2 | evidence
Q: In Chambliss’s 1973 study, how did the delinquency rates of the Saints and Roughnecks compare?
A: They were about equal
Q: What class-linked difference in police treatment did Chambliss report between the two groups?
A: The lower-class Roughnecks faced more police attention and sanctions
Q: What limits generalising Chambliss’s findings to all social classes?
A: He studied only two small groups in one high school community
