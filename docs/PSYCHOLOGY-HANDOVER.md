`prompts/subject-question-bank-prompt-v2.md` (project files). The AQA pipeline is
adapted from Geography (`docs/GEOGRAPHY-HANDOVER.md`) and Politics. Nothing is called
complete while public booklets are still queued.

### Specification

`data/specs/AQA_ALEVEL_PSYCH_7182.json` is generated by `tools/psychology/build_spec.py`
from AQA-7181-7182-SP-2025-v1.4.pdf (SHA256 `db561ba9…6ad4d`; other revisions refused).

- AQA prints content sections (4.1.1 Social influence … 4.3.10 Addiction, and research
  methods 4.2.3, 4.2.3.1-4.2.3.3) as unnumbered bullets. The **84 leaves** are those
  bullets in printed order, coded by position: `4.1.1 (2)` = id `AQA_PSYCH_4.1.1_b2`.
  The position is our identity, not a code AQA prints.
- Tree: Paper 1/2/3 → the seeded topic ids kept as groups (`AQA_PSYCH_SOCIAL` …
  `AQA_PSYCH_ISSUES`) plus new option groups `AQA_PSYCH_COGDEV`, `AQA_PSYCH_SCHIZ`,
  `AQA_PSYCH_FORENSIC` (kind `option`). Out-of-scope options are listed in
  `options_not_in_scope`, not built.
- AS 7181 (spec section 3) repeats 48 of the bullets (text similarity ≥ 0.85). Those are
  `as_reference` / `year_12_points`: the co-teaching Year 12 basis, not an AQA teaching order.
- Seeded leaf ids migrate (`legacy_ids` → `js/config/spec-id-migrations.js`). Seeded
  ids for content Version 1.4 removed (caregiver-infant interactions, biological
  rhythms) and for out-of-route options have no migration.

### Extraction

`tools/psychology/extract.py` (layout v1): one unit per AQA label ("0 1", "0 2 . 1",
"1 2 . 10"), from the label to its tariff; multiple-choice options after the tariff
stay with the unit. **All 35 booklets reconcile to their cover maximum with 0
exceptions (894 units, 702 in route).**

- Shared items ("Read the examples in Table 1 then answer Questions 01 and 02") are
  their own unscored context image, attached to the questions they name (or to "the
  questions that follow" in the same section/topic), not to the first question's crop.
- Scenario context: a question continuing an earlier question's scenario ("the
  psychologist", a named person, a Table printed earlier) records `context_units`
  with reasons; answer references ("your graph", "your answer to 03.2") are flagged
  for the dependency gate. The model review confirms or overrides each one.
- Paper 3 choice groups come from "Choose one topic from Section B" and "Topic: X"
  headings; the paper maximum counts one topic per section.
- Specimen booklets print boxed labels a few points off their text line; a row holding
  only a label absorbs the level text row.
- **Checked tariff misprint:** specimen set 1 Paper 3 Gender Q14 prints [1 mark]; the
  specimen mark scheme gives 2 (AO3 = 2). Override pinned to the source SHA256 in
  `data/imports/psychology/tariff-overrides.json` (Gender is out of route anyway).
- PDF geometry: CropBox equals MediaBox on every source.
- `tools/psychology/schemes.py` indexes every unit's scheme in all 35 mark schemes;
  every "Marks for this question: AO… = n" total matches the unit's tariff.

### Reproduce

```sh
R=../psychology-research
curl -o $R/page.html https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/psychology/a-level/psychology-7182/assessment-resources
curl -o $R/page-as.html https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/psychology/as-level/psychology-7181/assessment-resources
python3 tools/psychology/resources.py $R/page.html $R/page-as.html > $R/records.json
python3 tools/psychology/fetch.py $R/records.json --dest $R/sources
python3 tools/psychology/inventory.py $R/records.json --sources $R/sources
curl -o $R/spec.pdf https://cdn.sanity.io/files/p28bar15/green/661250c1cb8bd3586f93ddb48c89a3218a3913ca.pdf
python3 tools/psychology/build_spec.py $R/spec.pdf
for q in 7182 7181; do python3 tools/psychology/extract.py data/imports/psychology/inventory/$q-inventory.json --sources $R/sources --output $R/candidates; done
python3 tools/psychology/reviews.py prepare $R
```
