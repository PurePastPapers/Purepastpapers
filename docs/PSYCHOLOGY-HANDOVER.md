# AQA A-level Psychology 7182 (and AS 7181) question bank: handover

Live working notes for the AQA Psychology import (`AQA_ALEVEL_PSYCH_7182`), run under
`prompts/subject-question-bank-prompt-v2.md` (project files). The AQA pipeline is
adapted from Geography (`docs/GEOGRAPHY-HANDOVER.md`) and Politics. Nothing is called
complete while public booklets are still queued.

## Run settings and owner decisions (16 November 2026)

- Network: the owner's "purepastpapers" cloud environment. Hosts used:
  `www.aqa.org.uk`, `filestore.aqa.org.uk`, `cdn.sanity.io` (all reachable).
- Rights: the owner confirmed AQA permission on 16 November 2026 (covers AQA papers).
- Route (from the owner's school prospectus, The Reach Free School Sixth Form 2025/26):
  Papers 1 and 2 in full; Paper 3 Section A (issues and debates) plus the options
  **Cognition and development** (B), **Schizophrenia** (C) and **Forensic psychology** (D).
  Relationships, Gender, Eating behaviour, Stress, Aggression and Addiction are out of
  scope and are not published (they are still extracted so every booklet reconciles).
- AS 7181 papers are included (owner, 16 November 2026).
- Textbook: none. Navigation is the official specification only
  (textbook organisation: not applicable unless the owner supplies one).
- **Updated specification.** AQA's current specification is Version 1.4
  (1 September 2025): first teaching 2025, first AS exams 2026, first A-level exams 2027.
  Every public past paper (2017-2025) was set on the earlier version. Owner decision
  (16 November 2026): questions that need content Version 1.4 no longer includes are
  **left out and listed** with the reason (`excluded_questions`, coverage.json).
- Publishing: tested checkpoints go straight to `main` (owner, 16 November 2026).
- Reviews are model reviews (`reviewer_type: model`), never teacher reviews.

## Checkpoint 1: specification, inventory, extraction (16 November 2026)

### Specification

`data/specs/AQA_ALEVEL_PSYCH_7182.json` is generated by `tools/psychology/build_spec.py`
from AQA-7181-7182-SP-2025-v1.4.pdf (SHA256 `db561ba9…6ad4d`; other revisions refused).

- AQA prints content sections (4.1.1 Social influence … 4.3.10 Addiction, and research
  methods 4.2.3, 4.2.3.1-4.2.3.3) as unnumbered bullets. The **84 leaves** are those
  bullets in printed order, coded by position: `4.1.1 (2)` = id `AQA_PSYCH_4.1.1_b2`.
  The position is our identity, not a code AQA prints.
- Tree: Paper 1/2/3 → the seeded topic ids kept as groups (`AQA_PSYCH_SOCIAL` …
  `AQA_PSYCH_ISSUES`) plus new option groups `AQA_PSYCH_COGDEV`, `AQA_PSYCH_SCHIZ`,
  `AQA_PSYCH_FORENSIC` (kind `option`). Out-of-scope options are listed in
  `options_not_in_scope`, not built.
- AS 7181 (spec section 3) repeats 48 of the bullets (text similarity ≥ 0.85). Those are
  `as_reference` / `year_12_points`: the co-teaching Year 12 basis, not an AQA teaching order.
- Seeded leaf ids migrate (`legacy_ids` → `js/config/spec-id-migrations.js`). Seeded
  ids for content Version 1.4 removed (caregiver-infant interactions, biological
  rhythms) and for out-of-route options have no migration.

### Extraction

`tools/psychology/extract.py` (layout v1): one unit per AQA label ("0 1", "0 2 . 1",
"1 2 . 10"), from the label to its tariff; multiple-choice options after the tariff
stay with the unit. **All 35 booklets reconcile to their cover maximum with 0
exceptions (894 units, 702 in route).**

- Shared items ("Read the examples in Table 1 then answer Questions 01 and 02") are
  their own unscored context image, attached to the questions they name (or to "the
  questions that follow" in the same section/topic), not to the first question's crop.
- Scenario context: a question continuing an earlier question's scenario ("the
  psychologist", a named person, a Table printed earlier) records `context_units`
  with reasons; answer references ("your graph", "your answer to 03.2") are flagged
  for the dependency gate. The model review confirms or overrides each one.
- Paper 3 choice groups come from "Choose one topic from Section B" and "Topic: X"
  headings; the paper maximum counts one topic per section.
- Specimen booklets print boxed labels a few points off their text line; a row holding
  only a label absorbs the level text row.
- **Checked tariff misprint:** specimen set 1 Paper 3 Gender Q14 prints [1 mark]; the
  specimen mark scheme gives 2 (AO3 = 2). Override pinned to the source SHA256 in
  `data/imports/psychology/tariff-overrides.json` (Gender is out of route anyway).
- PDF geometry: CropBox equals MediaBox on every source.
- `tools/psychology/schemes.py` indexes every unit's scheme in all 35 mark schemes;
  every "Marks for this question: AO… = n" total matches the unit's tariff.


## Checkpoint 2: model review and build (16 November 2026)

**Model review** (`reviewer_type: model`, never a teacher review). One line per in-route unit
in `data/imports/psychology/review-src/<PAPER>.txt` (format in `tools/psychology/reviews.py`),
compiled to `data/imports/psychology/reviews/`. 702 in-route units: **653 published**, **49 left
out** because they need content Version 1.4 no longer includes (owner decision). Removed
content met: types of long-term memory; caregiver-infant interactions (reciprocity,
interactional synchrony, Schaffer's stages, role of the father); Zimbardo and conformity to
social roles; identification; social change; biological rhythms; Wundt and introspection
(including "emergence of psychology as a science"); token economies; psychodynamic
explanations of offending; atavistic form. Kept with a lead note: questions where removed
content is only one listed choice or a distractor (biological offending listing atavistic
form; matching items with "identification" as a distractor), validity/reliability of
schizophrenia diagnosis (v1.4 frames it as co-morbidity, bias and symptom overlap), theory
of mind and autism, and a research-design question set in a biological-rhythms study.
192 Paper 3 option units outside the route are listed, not published.

**Build** (`tools/psychology/build.py`, `finalize.py`):
- Unit crop, part-only scheme crop (to the next unit's label; stops at the Assessment
  Objective Grid / copyright footer), lossless WebP.
- Unscored context: the part's shared stem (`stem_image_url`); shared items as figures
  ("Shared information printed before Question N"); earlier questions whose scenario the
  unit continues ("Question N (this question continues its scenario)").
- Dependency gate: a unit that builds on an earlier answer has `depends_on_question_ids`,
  a `shared_stem_html` note and the earlier question shown. Because the schema keeps
  dependencies inside one parent, a dependent question joins its source's group
  (e.g. 2025 P1 Q17 has parent Q16 and `part_label` "17").
- Reconciliation per booklet: published + removed-content marks = cover maximum
  (Paper 3: Section A plus one route topic per option section).
- Content year: AS 7181 units Year 12; A-level units Year 12 only when every primary leaf is
  AS-shared, else Year 13 (440 / 213). Curriculum mapping `aqa-7182-7181-co-teaching-years`.
- Difficulty: 181 easy, 355 medium, 117 hard. Every one of the 84 leaves has a primary unit.
- Extraction fix: an AQA "This is a repeat of information" stem no longer replaces the full
  item printed first (AS specimen Paper 2 Q12).

Tests: `tests/psychology-bank.test.mjs` (7 pass). `npm test` otherwise unchanged: the same 7
UI tests fail on the main baseline (c353026). Browser check: `tools/browser-psychology-bank.cjs`.
