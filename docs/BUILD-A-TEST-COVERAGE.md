## Partial coverage

- **Edexcel A-level Biology (9BN0), June 2025 Paper 3 Q8.** All 11 units require the unpublished pre-release article and are excluded from custom tests.
- **OCR AS Chemistry (H032), June 2017 Paper 2.** Q4(b)(ii) and Q5(c) require source material removed from the public paper. The remaining supported units can be drawn.
- **AQA A-level Spanish (7692).** Paper 1 and Paper 2 have usable written questions, including on-screen listening with official audio. Paper 3 speaking is a partner-practice resources route. The 2018 and 2019 written papers lack verified public sources. Printable tests keep supported PDF resources as links. An audio-dependent selection requires the original recording and cannot become a standalone question PDF.
- **OCR Level 3.** Applied Business, Information Technology, Sport and Applied Science have no indexed attemptable questions in this snapshot. The builder becomes available once an enabled bank supplies an attemptable official question.
- Rows showing zero units are catalogue or paper listings, empty banks, or coursework-only subjects. Times, topics, tiers and module routes narrow the builder pool for the current student.

Official full-paper choice and scoring rules continue to apply when sitting full papers. Custom tests assemble independently attemptable questions, including essays, and show the total for the selected questions.
