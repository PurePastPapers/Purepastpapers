# AQA A-level Geography 7037 question bank: handover

Live working notes for the AQA A-level Geography import (`AQA_ALEVEL_GEOG_7037`), run
under `docs/prompts/SUBJECT-QUESTION-BANK-AGENT.md`. The AQA pipeline is adapted from
Politics (`docs/POLITICS-HANDOVER.md`). Nothing is called complete while public
booklets are still queued.

## Run settings

- Coordination: Claude (Opus) lead, which reviews every ambiguous tag, option boundary and
  difficulty. Extraction/tagging batches: Claude (Sonnet) subagents at medium effort,
  each owning distinct booklet files. These stand in for the brief's "Sol High" and
  "Luna Medium" routing. All reviews are `reviewer_type: model`, never teacher review.
- Network: AQA hosts (`www.aqa.org.uk`, `filestore.aqa.org.uk`, `cdn.sanity.io`) are
  allowed in the owner's personal "Purepastpapers" cloud environment (16 November 2026).
- Rights: on 16 November 2026 the owner confirmed their AQA permission also covers
  republishing AQA Geography 7037/7036 papers and mark schemes.
- Textbook: none supplied. Navigation uses the official AQA specification only.

## Checkpoint 1: specification (16 November 2026)

**Status: no Geography questions are imported yet.** The 9 existing Geography rows
are hidden demo records (now tagged to official leaves).

`data/specs/AQA_ALEVEL_GEOG_7037.json` is generated by `tools/geography/build_spec.py`
from AQA's 7037 specification (Version 1.2, 2 September 2019, SHA256 `f7fac6d2…7fca72`)
and AS 7036 specification (Version 1.0, 2 June 2016, SHA256 `f907ae02…217d4c51`). The
build refuses any other revision.

- **83 leaves**: the 75 finest numbered content sections (3.1.1.1 to 3.2.5.7, with
  3.2.1.5 and 3.2.1.5.1 both kept and 3.2.2.2 split into 3.2.2.2.1/3.2.2.2.2 as AQA
  prints it), fieldwork requirements 3.3.1, the skills checklist (3.4.1, 3.4.2.1 to
  3.4.2.5) and one AS-only leaf (below). Each leaf stores AQA's content text verbatim
  with source pages and its paper-section route.
- **Routes**: Paper 1 = A water and carbon (compulsory, 36), B one of hot deserts /
  coasts / glaciers (36), C one of hazards / ecosystems under stress (48). Paper 2 =
  A global systems (36), B changing places (36, compulsory), C one of urban /
  population / resources (48). Optional topics are `kind: "option"` with that route.
  Paper 3 is the NEA (coursework): not imported as questions.
- **AS 7036** numbers the same content differently (AS 3.1.2 Coasts = A-level 3.1.3;
  AS 3.3.1 Hazards = A-level 3.1.5; AS 3.3.2 Urban = A-level 3.2.3; AS 3.2.1 Changing
  places = A-level 3.2.2; AS 3.5 skills = A-level 3.4). `as_section_map` records all
  47 AS sections and each A-level leaf records its AS code and normalised text
  similarity (all 1.0 except ICT skills 3.4.2.5 at 0.85, wording only).
- **AS fieldwork** (7036 3.4) is examined in AS Paper 2 Section B and lists the
  enquiry stages, which the A level assesses only through the NEA (similarity to
  A-level 3.3.1 is 0.52). It is therefore its own leaf `AQA_GEOG_AS_3.4`, scoped to 7036.
- The 56 seeded leaf ids migrate on load (`js/config/spec-id-migrations.js`, written
  by `tools/write_spec_migrations.py`). The seeded group ids are kept as topic ids.
- Linear A level: AQA prints no Year 12/13 order. Only the AS 7036 co-teachable
  content is evidence for a Year 12 pool; see checkpoint 3 for how it is used.

## Checkpoint 2: source inventory (16 November 2026)

**Status: no Geography questions are imported yet.**

`data/imports/geography/inventory/7037-inventory.json` and `7036-inventory.json` are
written by `tools/geography/inventory.py` from the JSON embedded in AQA's public
assessment-resources pages for 7037 and 7036 (file name, publisher sha1, size), parsed
by `tools/geography/resources.py`. `tools/geography/fetch.py` downloads every question
paper, mark scheme, insert (all variants), examiner report and sample commentary from
AQA's cdn.sanity.io asset URL into `../geography-research/sources` and checks the
`%PDF` header and AQA's sha1 (110 files, all verified). Modified question papers are
skipped (same questions). Maximum marks and durations come from each booklet's cover.

| Qualification | Series | QP | MS | Insert | Examiner report |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A level 7037 | Nov 2020, Nov 2021, Jun 2022, Jun 2023, Jun 2024, Jun 2025 | 2 each | 2 each | 2 each (mostly -CR) | all except Nov 2021 |
| A level 7037 | Sample set 1 | 2 (-CR, figures inline) | 2 | none | none (answers and commentary instead) |
| AS 7036 | Nov 2020, Jun 2022, Jun 2023, Jun 2024, Jun 2025 | 2 each | 2 each | standard QPs print figures inline | all except Nov 2020 |
| AS 7036 | Sample set 1 | 2 (-CR) | 2 | none | none (answers and commentary instead) |

26 booklets: 14 A level (12 exams + 2 samples), 12 AS (10 exams + 2 samples).

- **Adapted sitting:** AS June 2022 Paper 2 is printed as 65 marks, 1 hour 15 minutes
  (the specification says 80 marks, 1 hour 30). The cover values are used.
- **Not public:** A-level June 2018 and June 2019, AS June 2017 to June 2019 and AS
  November 2021 are not on AQA's public pages. No secure or teacher routes were used.
  June 2026 papers are not yet listed.
- **Copyright removed:** most A-level inserts are AQA's "-CR" versions with named
  figures replaced by a notice (for example June 2022 Paper 1 Figures 7a, 7b and 9).
  A unit whose required figure is removed will be excluded and recorded, not
  published incomplete.

### Reproduce

```sh
R=../geography-research
curl -o $R/page.html https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/geography/a-level/geography-7037/assessment-resources

```

## Checkpoint 3: extraction, figures and schemes (16 November 2026)

**Status: no Geography questions are published yet.** Candidates are in
`../geography-research/candidates/` (not committed; reproducible).

- `tools/geography/extract.py` (layout v2): one scored unit per AQA part label
  ("0 1 . 2", also printed "0 1 2"), ending at its "[n marks]" tariff. **All 26
  booklets reconcile to their cover maximum with 0 exceptions: 635 units.**
  - Choice groups come from the cover ("Answer either Question 2 or Question 3 or
    Question 4"); every option in a group carries the same total.
  - AS multiple choice: the A to D options are printed after "[1 mark]" (sometimes as
    flow-chart rows with the letter mid-option). They are kept with the part; all 72
    one-mark AS units have exactly four options.
  - A question-level label without a tariff (AS Paper 2 fieldwork scenarios, "0 3 A
    group of students...") is a **shared stem**: unscored context for every part.
  - Figures printed inline are indexed by heading. A part's own crop keeps only its own
    lines; figure rows it does not cite are dropped (AQA often prints a part's figure
    after its tariff, above the next part).
- `tools/geography/figures.py` indexes insert figures (rotated landscape pages are
  read with PDFium). `tools/geography/resolve.py` resolves every citation: 134 in the
  unit's own crop, 64 in a shared stem, 166 in the insert, 55 printed elsewhere in the
  paper, 0 unresolved.
- **Excluded: 35 units** whose cited figure (or own text) AQA removed for third-party
  copyright. No modified insert restores a removed figure (the one non-CR modified
  insert is an OS map key). They are recorded, not published.
- `tools/geography/schemes.py` finds each unit's scheme from its label row to the next
  (labels matched strictly in paper order, including one stacked label in AS Nov 2020
  Paper 2). All 26 schemes index all units; levels grids are included with their part.
- `tools/geography/reviews.py prepare` writes review packets (unit text, route, figures,
  official scheme excerpt); `compile` enforces route-scoped primary tags (an option's
  questions can only be tagged to that option plus skills 3.4.x; AS units only to
  AS-shared content or AS3.4).
