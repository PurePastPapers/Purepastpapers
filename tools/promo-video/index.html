A-Level Physics Spec 2.4 · 3 marks
A ball is projected at 18 m s−1 at 35° above the horizontal from the top of a 25 m cliff. Find the time it takes to hit the sea.
Your answer
u = 18 sin 35° = 10.3
−25 = 10.3t − 4.9t²
t = 2.1 s
redo it: t = 3.5 s
Mark scheme
M1vertical part: 18 sin 35°
M1s = ut + ½at² with s = −25
A1t = 3.5 s
